## Privacy Policy

This is a personal website hosted on [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/). This site itself does not set cookies, run analytics, show ads or host comments, and I do not collect personal information. Third-party services used on some pages are described below.

### Hosting

GitHub, which hosts this site, may log technical data such as your IP address when you visit. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

### Third-party content

Some pages load scripts, styles, fonts, map tiles or embedded content from third parties. When they do, your browser connects to those services, which can see your IP address and may set their own cookies under their own privacy policies:

* Code libraries and icons from public CDNs: [jsDelivr](https://www.jsdelivr.com/terms/privacy-policy-jsdelivr-net), [cdnjs (Cloudflare)](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/), code.jquery.com, d3js.org and cdn.plot.ly (for example on the blog posts with maps and charts, and on the CrossFit dashboard).
* [Google Fonts](https://policies.google.com/privacy) on a few pages (for example the Travel Score project and the Pi Day post).
* Embedded [Google My Maps](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Spotify](https://www.spotify.com/legal/privacy-policy/) players on the [Funfolio](/others/) page, and Google Maps links or embeds in some travel posts.
* OpenStreetMap-based map tiles on the interactive map posts (for example the Aachen fountain post).

If you prefer, you can block third-party requests and cookies in your browser settings; the main text of every page still works without them.
